Backup & Restore

Important: This app normally stores data on the current device/browser. To move all your data to another phone, tablet, laptop, or to email/WhatsApp, use Create Portable App With All Data. The downloaded HTML contains a copy of your current inventory, stock entries, movements, suppliers, documents, signatures, feedback and settings. Open that portable HTML on the other device and your data will be imported automatically. Keep a separate JSON backup as an additional safety copy.
